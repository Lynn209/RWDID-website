Input guide

Preparing an RWDID assessment

This guide explains how to enter the 15-component questionnaire for the browser calculator.

1. Intake period

The UK Biobank dietary questions are based on the previous 24 hours. The research pipeline used available repeated dietary-recall instances. For this public calculator, enter your average intake on a typical day rather than a single unusually high- or low-intake day.

2. Portion and response units

Most questions use the original UK Biobank response categories, converted to portions per day using the study's item-level encoding. Where the input is numeric grams, enter grams per day; the calculator divides by the Table S1 portion size to obtain portions per day.

3. No intake versus missing

Select an explicit zero response or use “No intake from this food group” only when no listed item in that group was consumed. A blank field is missing and is not automatically treated as zero. The calculator requires a response for every active item.

4. Refined-grains mapping

The 85-row audit retains four white-bread pseudo-fields. The frozen data-preparation script sequentially overwrites the same derived variable; the final retained assignment is the Bread roll item selected through Field ID 20094. The calculator follows the frozen active 82-input mapping.

The 15 frozen dietary components

Fruit and vegetables

Citrus fruits · Red/orange vegetables · Other vegetables · Potatoes

Grains and animal foods

Refined grains · Eggs · Low-fat dairy · Red meats · Processed meats · Fish and seafood

Beverages

Coffee · Fruit juices · Sweetened beverages · Water · Wine

Scoring output

RWDID: binary β-weighted score, 0–1. RWDID-C: continuous equal-weight score, 0–15.
