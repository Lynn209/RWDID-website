Input guide

Use these brief rules when entering dietary intake.

Typical daily intake

Enter the average amount consumed on a typical day, rather than an unusually high- or low-intake day.

Zero intake

Select “0 · Not consumed” for an item not consumed. Use “No intake from this food group” only when none of its listed items were consumed.

Gram-based items

For fields requiring grams, enter grams per day. The calculator converts grams to portions using the listed portion size.

Missing responses

Do not leave an item blank. Missing responses are not treated as zero, and no imputation is performed.
